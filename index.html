The direction

Less clutter. More control.

Kestrel is being shaped around the parts of a desktop that matter every day: a coherent look, sensible software choices, and a system you can actually understand and change.

◈

A considered desktop

A modern, dark visual identity with clear typography, restrained color, and a familiar desktop layout.

⌘

Linux without the mystery

Practical defaults and transparent choices, without pretending that a theme alone makes a distribution.

↗

Made to evolve

Installer, branding, and system decisions are still being worked on. The project can grow without promising vaporware.
